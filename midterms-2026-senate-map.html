The ten anti-war-central winners

Races where the winner's stated priorities put ending the Iran war first.

Adam Hamilton

Kansas · flip · first Dem senator since 1932

Methodist pastor. Said his first priority to lower Kansans' costs would be to end the war, which nearly doubled diesel and fertilizer prices for farmers. Entered the race after Washington "got into a war with Iran."

The Guardian · AP · Fox 4 KC · The Atlantic

Josh Turek

Iowa · flip · open seat (Ernst retired)

Paralympic gold medalist, state lawmaker, "generational change." Attacked Hinson's six votes to continue the war, tied it to gas prices, named ending it an early priority; earlier called for conditions on aid to Israel over Gaza.

NBC News · Des Moines Register · C-SPAN · The Gazette

Troy Jackson

Maine · flip · defeats 30-year incumbent Collins

Said Congress should shut off funding for the war to force its end, and attacked Collins for failing to stand up to it.

Jewish Insider · Jackson for Maine

Roy Cooper

North Carolina · flip · defeats Whatley

Former governor. Pledged in his victory speech to bring down gas and grocery costs and end the war in Iran.

WECT · Al Jazeera

Sherrod Brown

Ohio (special) · flip · defeats Husted

Return to the Senate with an anti-war message as a campaign cornerstone; called the war unnecessary, destructive and expensive.

Signal Ohio · Signal Cleveland

James Talarico

Texas · flip · defeats Paxton · youngest senator

His published platform: end the "forever war" in Iran driving up gas and grocery costs. Won against Elon Musk's $212 million effort.

Talarico campaign site · Houston Chronicle

Annie Andrews

South Carolina (special) · flip · seat open after Lindsey Graham's death

Pediatrician. Called the war "illegal," criticized its effect on gas prices, and said her work in the Senate starts with helping extricate the US from it.

The Guardian · Post and Courier

Abdul El-Sayed

Michigan · Dem hold

Ran an anti-war "Michigan first" ad; opposing funding for the Iran war was a key platform tenet.

Washington Times · The Intercept

Peggy Flanagan

Minnesota · Dem hold (special)

Opposed the war from the outset, calling it illegal, and pledged to fight to end it and bring down gas prices.

Star Tribune · Minnesota DFL

John Hickenlooper

Colorado · Dem hold

Told a public voter guide his highest priority is to stop the war in Iran.

Colorado Public Radio voter guide

Read the map with care. The Democratic Party's own post-election assessment said its Senate candidates were laser-focused on "high gas prices, an unpopular war in Iran and stubbornly high inflation." Every one of the seven seats the party flipped was won by a candidate who ran against the war, and three Democratic incumbents did the same. But the war was not the whole story on either side: ten Democratic winners raised it without centering it, and no Republican winner campaigned against it. The badge marks where ending the war was the campaign, not just part of it. Race counts as of November 6, 2026; Alaska and Mississippi were uncalled and counts elsewhere were still being finalized.
